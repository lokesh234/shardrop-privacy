At a glance

Your progress stays on your phone

Scores, shards, skins, missions and settings are saved only on your device.

We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

01Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, Prismfall saves the following on your iPhone:

  • Best scores, unlocked maps and mastery stars
  • Shards, owned and selected skins
  • Missions, daily streaks and Daily Prism results
  • Your settings for music, sound and haptics

This information never leaves your device through Prismfall, and we (the developer) can't see it. It's included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone if you make them, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes it.

We do not run our own servers, and Prismfall does not include any analytics service of its own.

02Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, Prismfall sends your scores, Daily Prism results and achievements to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and to your friends. Your Game Center nickname and profile are managed by Apple, and we don't receive your Apple ID or email.

Game Center is optional. You can sign out in Settings → Game Center and the game works the same. Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

03Advertising

Prismfall shows ads to stay free: occasional full-screen ads between runs, and optional ads you can choose to watch for bonus shards. Ads are delivered by AppLovin MAX, which may work with other ad networks (its “mediation partners”) to fill each ad.

What AppLovin and its partners may receive

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)
  • Device and app information: device model, iOS version, language, time zone, screen size, network type, app version
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate (city-level) location
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, watched, skipped or tapped

Why

To choose and deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, attribute installs, and detect fraud. If you allow tracking, ads may also be personalized based on your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

Tracking is your choice. Prismfall asks for permission with Apple's standard prompt before any tracking. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared, and you'll still see ads; they just won't be personalized using cross-app activity.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it before ads use your data for personalization.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Learn more about how AppLovin handles data in the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

05Your choices

  1. Stop trackingGo to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off Prismfall, or turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”.
  2. Limit personalized Apple adsGo to Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  3. Leave Game CenterSign out in Settings → Game Center. Your progress on the device is kept.
  4. Erase everythingDelete Prismfall from your iPhone. All game data stored by the app is removed.

06Children

Prismfall is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we'll help remove it.

07Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, UK and US states such as California, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is processed about you, and get a copy
  • Ask for it to be corrected or deleted
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

Because we don't hold your data on our own servers, the fastest way to exercise most of these is through the settings above. For data held by our ad partner, you can also contact AppLovin directly through its privacy policy. You're always welcome to contact us, and we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

For users in the EEA and UK: the legal basis for advertising that uses your data is your consent; for non-personalized ads and fraud prevention, it is our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and safe.

08Retention & security

Data saved on your device stays until you delete the app. Game Center data is kept by Apple under its policies. AppLovin and its partners keep advertising data for the periods set out in their own policies. Information sent to them is transmitted over encrypted connections.

09Changes to this policy

If the way Prismfall handles information changes, for example if we add a new partner, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
